# Scientific evidence for repository context retrieval

The useful distinction is between representation, retrieval policy, and the interface presented to an agent. A paper demonstrating one does not automatically validate the others. The studies below motivate experiments; their numbers are not directly comparable across models, workloads, or accounting methods.

| Study and publication status | Method and evaluated workload | Reported finding | Consequence and limitation for graf | Reproduction material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Results concern the whole repair pipeline, not isolated retrieval superiority. | [Author implementation](https://github.com/OpenAutoCoder/Agentless) |

| [GraphRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130), 2024 manuscript | Entity graph and community summaries; corpus-wide sensemaking | Better comprehensiveness/diversity than the evaluated naive RAG baseline | Include global architecture questions. | [CodeGrep](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05886v1), August 2026 preprint | Trained 14B retrieval agent injected into a frozen OpenHands agent; SWE-bench Verified | Own-resolved-set tokens fall 19%; paired solved instances fall 15%; overall resolution rises 1.2 percentage points | Precision can matter more than candidate volume. | [Agent Retrieval Bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24882v1), July 2026 preprint | 427 samples, 25 repositories; tests, comment context, traces, edit consequences, no-gold cases | Different families win different metrics; RepoMap leads budgeted context yield at 8K in that evaluation | Use workflow-specific and budget-specific metrics, including insufficient evidence. ## Strength of the inference

There is evidence for hierarchy, selective retrieval, structural relationships, and interface design. There is no controlled head-to-head result here establishing that the proposed graf combination beats the pinned Graphify version. The 2026 papers are treated as preprints, and this project does not reproduce their training or headline results.

The working hypothesis is that cheap lexical/symbol lookup, optional semantic search, and targeted relationship expansion can complement each other. Architecture summaries and executable source excerpts serve different needs. A tree is itself a restricted graph; symbol reference tables also encode relationships. The engineering question is which relations to maintain and retrieve, rather than whether all graphs should be eliminated.

## Evidence for the next retrieval decisions

Reviewed 2026-10-18 for four open questions from the code audit: when to stop delivering ranked windows, how to rank files from window scores, how to tokenize queries and code, and how to evaluate a change against Graphify. Each source was checked against its arXiv, ACL Anthology, ACM, or author page. The claims with the largest effect on a decision were re-read in the primary text: the SWE-bench context table, the RLT reproduction's conclusions, Adaptive-k, BLUiR, SweRank, the agentic-eval variance study, and the context-length study. Entries marked *bibliographic record only* were not read in full. None of these results were reproduced here. No source evaluates graf's exact setting: training-free BM25 over 24-line windows, delivered to a coding agent under a token budget.

### Stopping before the budget is full

| Study and status | Method and workload | Reported finding | Consequence and limitation for graf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Jimenez et al., SWE-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770), ICLR 2024 | Whole-file BM25 context at 13K/27K/50K tokens; single-shot issue resolution | Claude 2 resolved 1.96%, 1.87%, 1.22% as average oracle-file recall rose 29.58, 44.41, 51.06; "models perform best on the shortest context window" | The most direct code evidence that more retrieved context can lower task success while recall rises. 2023 models, no agent loop, whole files rather than windows. |
| [Du et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05381), Findings of EMNLP 2025 | Five LLMs; math, QA, coding; length varied with retrieval held perfect | 13.9%–85% degradation as input length increases | Length itself has a cost, independent of distractors. Synthetic length, not retrieved code. |
| [Jin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05983), ICLR 2025 per proceedings listing; arXiv lists no venue | Long-context LLMs with growing numbers of retrieved passages | Quality first rises, then falls; hard negatives drive the decline | Lexically similar but irrelevant code windows are hard negatives. QA, not code. |
| [Meng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18185), SIGIR 2024 | Reproduces eight ranked-list truncation methods with BM25 and dense retrievers before LLM re-ranking; TREC DL 2019/2020 | "supervised RLT methods do not demonstrate a clear advantage over their unsupervised counterparts"; "findings on RLT do not generalize well to this new setup"; fixed depths approximate the trade-off | A simple fixed rule is a defensible starting point. Truncation results transfer poorly across pipelines, so graf must measure its own. |
| [Taguchi et al., Adaptive-k](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08479), EMNLP 2025 | Cut at the largest gap in sorted similarity scores, plus a fixed buffer; no training or extra LLM calls; long-context QA | "up to 10x fewer tokens than full-context input, yet still retrieves 70% of relevant passages" | The most portable deterministic rule. It used dense scores, not BM25, and about 30% recall loss would fail a no-regression quality gate. |
| [Arampatzis et al.](https://dare.uva.nl/id/a874f6a0-2d62-40de-b24f-6f0608f39fbe), SIGIR 2009 | Per-query normal/exponential score-distribution models choose a cutoff; TREC Legal | Truncated models were significantly better than untruncated ones | Score-only and unsupervised, but fitting is fragile on short candidate lists. Whether BM25 code-window scores follow these distributions is untested. |
| [Wu et al., Repoformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10059), ICML 2024 | Learned retrieval gating for repository completion | Many retrieved contexts are unhelpful or harmful | Supports delivering little or nothing for some requests. The gating policy is trained. |

### Ranking files from window scores

| Study and status | Method and workload | Reported finding | Consequence and limitation for graf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Reddy et al., SweRank](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07849), ICLR 2026 | Retrieve-and-rerank issue localization; file score is the maximum function score | SWE-Bench-Lite file Acc@1/3/5: BM25 38.69/51.82/61.68; SweRankEmbed-Large Acc@5 94.16 | The state of the art uses max aggregation for files. Its gain over BM25 comes from trained models, and Acc@k requires all relevant files, unlike graf's recall. |
| [Dai and Callan](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09217), SIGIR 2019 | BERT passage scores aggregated as first, max, or sum passage; Robust04, ClueWeb09-B | MaxP is equal to or slightly above SumP on most settings | Weak evidence that sum aggregation will not beat max. Neural scores, web text. |
| [Akkalyoncu Yilmaz et al.](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1352/), EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019 | Document score combined with weighted top-n sentence scores | Up to the top three sentences help; more does not | Directly informs a document-plus-damped-top-k file score. The weights were tuned per collection, so graf must fix them before evaluation. |
| [Saha et al., BLUiR](https://ischool.utexas.edu/ml/papers/saha-ase13.pdf), ASE 2013 | Training-free structured retrieval over class, method, variable, and comment fields; Java bug localization | Structure improved MAP and MRR overall; AspectJ found slightly fewer bugs in the top 5 and top 10 | The strongest training-free evidence for identifier and declaration fields. Losses on individual projects occur. |
| [Robertson and Zaragoza](https://staff.city.ac.uk/~sbrp622/papers/foundations_bm25_review.pdf), FnTIR 2009 | Derivation of BM25F | Combine weighted field term frequencies before saturation, rather than adding per-field scores | graf adds path terms to window text, which is equivalent to an unweighted path field. Published field weights are web-tuned and do not transfer to code. |
| [Fehr et al., CoRet](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-short.62/), ACL 2025 | Dense code-editing retriever using repository structure | Adding paths to chunks helps the trained model; the paper body reports minimal difference for BM25 (abstract re-read; this detail not re-read) | Plain path concatenation gives BM25 little. A weighted path field is untested. |

### Tokenization and stopwords

| Study and status | Method and workload | Reported finding | Consequence and limitation for graf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Saha et al., BLUiR](https://ischool.utexas.edu/ml/papers/saha-ase13.pdf), ASE 2013 | Index full identifiers as well as split tokens | "the addition of exact identifier names improved the accuracy for three of four subject systems"; "any stopword removed is almost certain to hurt matching for some particular query" | Supports indexing compound identifiers alongside their parts, and argues against request-phrasing stopwords. Java bug reports that contain identifiers. The authors also re-tuned k1/b for this task. |
| [Dit et al.](https://www.cs.wm.edu/~denys/pubs/ICPC2011-IdentifierSplitting.pdf), ICPC 2011 | CamelCase vs Samurai vs oracle splitting for feature location | Samurai was never significantly better than CamelCase splitting | graf's rule-based splitting is unlikely to be the bottleneck. LSI on small Java corpora. |
| [Fan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10439), NLBSE 2023 workshop | Domain stopwords derived from corpus statistics | Domain lists improved most measured SE text-processing outcomes | Domain lists can help when derived from corpus statistics. graf's list appears derived from request phrasing instead. |
| [Hill et al.](https://engineering.purdue.edu/RVL/Publications/SCAM12.pdf), SCAM 2012 | Stemming for concern and bug location in Java | Effects depend on the stemmer, weighting, and query | Stemming is low priority and should be expected to have no net effect. |
| [Li et al., CoIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.02883), ACL 2025 | Ten retrievers across code-retrieval tasks | BM25 ranks last on average, strong where queries share code vocabulary | Lexical retrieval needs shared vocabulary. Chunk similarity tasks, not repository navigation. |

### Evaluating a change against Graphify

| Study and status | Recommendation | Evidence | Consequence and limitation for graf |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Smucker et al.](https://ciir-publications.cs.umass.edu/getpdf.php?id=744), CIKM 2007; [Urbano et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.11096), SIGIR 2019 | Paired t-test or randomization test on per-query differences; avoid Wilcoxon and sign tests for mean effectiveness | Error-rate comparisons over TREC runs and large-scale simulation | Test the declared primary metric with a paired permutation test. Calibrated on TREC topics, not token counts. |
| [Lakens](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5502906/), SPPS 2017 | Equivalence and non-inferiority tests against a preset smallest effect of interest | Statistical primer | No test can confirm zero difference on every case. | [Buckley and Voorhees](https://doi.org/10.1145/1008992.1009000), SIGIR 2004 (*bibliographic record only*) | Measures robust to incomplete judgments | Standard measures degrade with incomplete judgments | Author-selected labels penalize valid unlabeled files. | [Sakai and Shang](https://research.nii.ac.jp/ntcir/workshop/OnlineProceedings12/pdf/evia/02-EVIA2016-SakaiT.pdf), EVIA 2016 | Choose topic count from pilot variance, effect size, α, and power | Worked examples need dozens of topics; 50 may not be sufficient | The current 30 queries can serve as a variance pilot, not as a confirmatory set. |
| [Miller](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640), 2024 preprint | Paired, clustered standard errors; plan sample size | Clustered errors can be much larger than naive ones | Two budgets per query are clustered: 30 units, not 60. |
| [Bjarnason et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07150), 2026 preprint | Several runs per task, power analysis, pass@k with pass^k | 60,000 SWE-bench Verified trajectories; single-run pass@1 varies by 2.2–6.0 points | Six tasks with three trials can detect gross failures only. Unreviewed preprint. |
| [Kapoor et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502), 2024 preprint | Proper holdouts; joint cost and accuracy reporting | Survey of agent benchmarks | Queries used for tuning are a development set. |
| [Georges et al.](https://dri.es/files/oopsla07-georges.pdf), OOPSLA 2007 | Many process invocations, confidence intervals across invocations | Java start-up measurement study | Three timing samples give very wide intervals. JVM study, not OS cache behavior. |
